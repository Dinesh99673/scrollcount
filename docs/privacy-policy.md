# Privacy Policy for ScrollCount

**Last updated: 16 September 2026**

ScrollCount is made by ProBuilder. This policy explains, in plain language,
exactly what the app does with information on your phone.

The short version: **ScrollCount collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no
internet access.**

## What the app does

ScrollCount counts how many Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts appear on your
screen, so you can see the number for yourself.

## What information is used

To notice a new reel, ScrollCount uses Android's Accessibility Service. It is
restricted to two apps — Instagram (`com.instagram.android`) and YouTube
(`com.google.android.youtube`) — and is not notified about anything you do in
any other app.

Within those two apps, the service checks one thing: whether the on-screen
layout belongs to the full-screen Reels or Shorts player, and whether a
different reel has appeared since it last looked. When it has, the app saves a
single row containing:

- the app it happened in (Instagram or YouTube), and
- the date and time it happened.

That is the entire record.

## What is never read or stored

ScrollCount does not read, store, transmit or process:

- messages, comments, captions or usernames,
- anything you type, including search terms and passwords,
- video, audio or images,
- the identity of any specific reel, short, account or creator,
- your contacts, location, photos, files, or any device identifier.

## Where your information is kept

Everything the app records stays in its own private storage on your phone.

ScrollCount does **not** request the `INTERNET` permission. The app is built so
this permission is explicitly removed from the final package, which means the
app is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere. Cloud backup of the
app's data is also switched off, so your counts are not copied to Google Drive.

## Who your information is shared with

Nobody. There are no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting services and
no third-party SDKs that collect data.

If you choose to use the "Share my week" feature, the app creates an image on
your phone and hands it to whichever app you pick. Nothing is uploaded by
ScrollCount itself, and the image contains only the numbers shown on the card.

## Notifications

ScrollCount can show you a notification when you reach half of, or all of, the
daily limit you set, plus an optional evening summary. These are generated on
your phone. You can turn them off in the app, or in your phone's settings.

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings → Reset all data** to permanently delete every reel the app has
counted. Uninstalling ScrollCount removes everything it has stored.

## Children

ScrollCount is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from anyone.
